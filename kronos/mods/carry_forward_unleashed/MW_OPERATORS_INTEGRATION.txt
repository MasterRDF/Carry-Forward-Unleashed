MW Operators integration for Carry Forward Unleashed 1.6

Original MW Operators mod, operator/skin list, menu fixes and native patch design: Maverick.
CFU integration: a startup companion and loader 4, preserving the tested weapon DLLs.

Adds 128 MWII/MWIII operators and 661 skins under CFU's Library entry.
The integration has been confirmed working in Zombies. Menu A-poses and reused
BO7 preview animations remain known issues and will be addressed in future work.

Install with BO7 and Kronos closed. Replace the included hid.dll in cod25, copy
the kronos folder, enable Carry Forward Unleashed in the Library, and enable
BO6 Operators in Kronos settings. Restart the game after changing the Library switch.

Migrating from standalone MW Operators: remove kronos/mods/mw_operators and
restore the original Kronos XINPUT9_1_0.dll from your backup or the Kronos updater.
Do not run the standalone and integrated copies together. This release does not
include or replace the Kronos DLL, settings, or your operator selections.

Requires stock Kronos v1.41 and the supported BO7 CL29269900 executable.
The companion checks the exact game/Kronos hashes, pinned roster and native
patch sites before applying the table expansion, targeted stale-string guard
and skin-rebuild changes in memory. Unknown versions or late initialization
are refused. The original Kronos DLL stays unchanged on disk.

hid.dll loader 4 initializes the operator companion before the existing weapon
DLLs. Switching CFU off before starting the game disables the integrated operator
companion, CFU weapon DLLs, Lua and scripts together.

Logs: kronos/Logs/mwcf_operators.log and mw2_guns_loader.log.
Expected operator message: enabled 128 added operators / 661 added skins.
The tested companion retains its original "integration trial 01" log prefix.
If it refuses initialization, inspect that log; visible menu rows alone do not
establish that the native operator patch is active.

The existing Pack-a-Punch and Max Ammo fixes are retained unchanged.
